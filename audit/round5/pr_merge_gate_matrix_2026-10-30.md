## Gate meanings

- **FOUNDATION:** Review Product Requirements, ADR status and canonical owners; no author can self-approve all cross-team contracts.
- **CONTRACT:** Accept exact version/Type/Schema/Event migration cross-owner, verify no conflicting unmerged Draft dependency.
- **SECURITY:** Actual deployed Browser/HTTP/MCP/Tool/Secrets/Parser security boundary must have an executable adversarial test.
- **INTEGRATION:** Execute the feature with real source-to-outbox/budget/identity/owner/consent and crash recovery, not just pure design.
- **QA:** Independent implementation-based tests and faults required; designed scenarios are not executed acceptance.
- **RELEASE:** Database checksum/backup/migrations/rolling upgrade and targeted benchmarks in actual implementation.
